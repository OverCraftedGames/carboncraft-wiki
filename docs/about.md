# About

OCG Survival is run by **OverCrafted Games**, a small independent game studio. It's the
first server on **OCG Network**, one home for every Minecraft server we run. More about the
network on [minecraft.ocgames.xyz](https://minecraft.ocgames.xyz/), and about the studio on
[ocgames.xyz](https://ocgames.xyz).

Until the network launch, OCG Survival was called **CarbonCraft**.

## Our servers

| Server | What it is | Address |
|---|---|---|
| **OCG Survival** | Hard-difficulty survival with a player-run economy, custom enchants, skills and factions | `play.ocgames.xyz` (Java and Bedrock) |
| **OCG Skyblock** | Start on a rock in the sky and build it into an empire | Coming soon |

## One store, every server

Everything on the [OCG Network store](https://minecraft.ocgames.xyz/store.html) works on
every OCG server. Each server decides its own perks.

- **[OCG+](https://minecraft.ocgames.xyz/plus.html)**: a monthly loot drop of Credits and a
  crate key, in three tiers (Charged, Overcharged and Supercharged). Cancel any time from
  your Tebex payment history; anything you've already received stays yours.
- **[Network ranks](https://minecraft.ocgames.xyz/ranks.html#survival)**: buy once, keep it
  for good. On OCG Survival, ranks give more homes, auction listings, player warps, claim
  blocks and extra commands. They stack with OCG+ and with your
  [vote tier](getting-started/voting.md).
- **[Credits](getting-started/money.md#credits)**: the network currency. One balance you can
  spend on any OCG server.

## Coming from CarbonCraft?

- **CarbonCoins are now Credits.** Your balance carried over 1:1. Check it with `/credits`.
- **Carbon+ (1, 3 or 6 months)** keeps every perk until the time you paid for runs out. It
  doesn't turn into OCG+ on its own, so pick an OCG+ tier if you want to carry on.
- **Carbon+ Lifetime** holders get the top network rank, **Emerald**, free and permanent,
  on every server.
- **Crate keys** haven't changed. Your keys and crates keep the same names.
