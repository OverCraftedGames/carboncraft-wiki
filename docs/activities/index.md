# Mining & fishing

Both run on their own progression systems, with levels, currencies, upgrades and bosses
that sit on top of vanilla Minecraft.

- [Mining](mining.md): every ore feeds your mining level and **Zeta**. Ores drop
  Carbon, gemstones and artifacts, and lead on to pickaxe Vessels and Oracle bosses.
- [Fishing](fishing.md): 157 custom fish earn fishing XP and **Entropy**, which you
  spend on rod augments, plus deliveries, totems and tournaments.
