# Cosmetics

Things that make you and your base look good and do nothing else. Most cosmetics are
bought with **CarbonCoins** in `/coinshop`. See [Money & currencies](../getting-started/money.md)
for how to earn them.

- [Tags](tags.md): a coloured tag next to your name in chat. Some you buy, some you
  earn by voting, logging in, opening crates or spending money.
- [Plushies](plushies.md): 111 mob plushies in three sizes, plus staff and Halloween
  packs whose plushies change pose when you right-click them.
