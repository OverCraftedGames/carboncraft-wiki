# Join messages

A join message replaces the plain "joined the game" line everyone sees when you log
in, and "left the game" when you leave. Most sets also play a sound for everyone when
you arrive.

## Picking one

Type `/joinmessage`, or open `/coinshop` → **Cosmetics** → **Join Messages**. Every set
is listed with its join and leave lines.

- **Left-click** a set you own to use it.
- **Right-click** any set, even a locked one, to preview it. Only you see the preview.
- **No Join Message** goes back to the normal messages.

You use one set at a time, and you keep every set you've unlocked.

## Buying a set

Sets with a price are bought with **CarbonCoins** straight from the menu. Left-click
one, then confirm. The coins are taken and the set is put on straight away.

| Set | Join message | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Spark | ✦ *you* sparked into the server. | 500 CarbonCoins |
| Deep Sea | 🎣 *you* surfaced from the deep. | 500 CarbonCoins |
| Clocked In | ⌚ *you* has clocked in. Productivity +1% | 750 CarbonCoins |
| Night Owl | ☾ *you* rose with the moon. | 750 CarbonCoins |
| Warp Drive | ⚡ *you* warped in from the void. | 1,000 CarbonCoins |
| Red Carpet | ♛ All rise for *you*. | 1,000 CarbonCoins |

The menu won't sell you a set you already own.

## Sets you earn

These can't be bought. Reach the goal and the set is yours: you get a message in chat
and it's ready to pick in the menu. Locked sets show your progress.

| Set | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Voice of the People | [Vote](../getting-started/voting.md) 300 times in total |
| Master Fisher | Reach [Fishing](../activities/fishing.md) level 100 |
| Master Miner | Reach [Mining](../activities/mining.md) level 100 |
| CarbonCorp Trusted | Reach **Trusted** standing with [CarbonCorp™](../syndicates/carboncorp.md) |
| Sentinel Trusted | Reach **Trusted** standing with the [ISD](../syndicates/isd.md) |
| Union Trusted | Reach **Trusted** standing with [The Union](../syndicates/union.md) |
| Rebel Trusted | Reach **Trusted** standing with [Vivec's Rebels](../syndicates/rebels.md) |

Trusted is 1,000 standing. See [The Syndicate](../syndicates/index.md) for how standing
works. Once you've earned a set you keep it, even if your standing drops later.

!!! tip "Earned sets can take a few minutes"
    Goals are checked when you join, when you open `/joinmessage` and every five
    minutes. Opening the menu is the quickest way to claim one.

Everyone has the **Classic** set.

!!! note "Relogging"
    If you leave and come back within 10 seconds, you get the normal join message
    instead of your set, so nobody can spam theirs.
