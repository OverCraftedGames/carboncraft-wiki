# Your first hour

The server runs on **Hard** difficulty. Mobs hit harder, and an unprotected base can be
griefed. This is the quickest way from spawn to a safe home.

## 1. Open the help menu

Type `/help` (or `/guide`). It's one screen with every feature on the server and live
stats for your account. Come back to it whenever you're unsure where something is.

## 2. Teleport into the wild

Spawn is protected, and you can't build near it. Type `/rtp` and choose
**Overworld Teleport** to land somewhere random in the wilderness.

| World | Claims | PvP | Resets | Unlocked at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overworld | yes | off | never | from the start |
| Nether | no | **on** | monthly | vote tier X |
| The End | no | **on** | monthly | vote tier XX |
| Legacy World | no | off | never | from the start |

The Legacy World is the old 1.21 CarbonCraft map, kept as a museum. You can explore it
in adventure mode but can't build there.

!!! warning "Build your base in the Overworld"
    Nothing in the Nether or End is safe, and both are wiped every month.

## 3. Claim your land

Nothing you build is protected until you claim it.

- The **first chest you place** automatically claims a small area around it.
- After that, hold a **golden shovel** and right-click two opposite corners to claim more.

You start with 100 claim blocks and earn 100 more per hour you play. See
[Claiming land](claiming-land.md) for the full guide.

## 4. Set a home

Stand in your base and type `/sethome`. From then on, `/home` brings you back. Vote
tiers raise the number of homes you can set.

## 5. Make some money

You start with **$200**. Prices are set by players, not by the server: sell what you
dig up at your own chest shop, or buy what you need from someone else's. Type
`/shophelp` for a step-by-step guide to setting up a shop, or read
[Money & currencies](money.md).

## 6. Vote every day

`/vote` lists the voting sites. Every vote counts toward your **vote tier**, which
brings free money, claim blocks, homes, warps and access to the Nether and End.
Voting on all six sites in one day also pays CarbonCoins. See [Voting & vote tiers](voting.md).

## 7. Check your daily stuff

`/quests` opens daily quests, monthly quests, story quests and your **daily login
reward**. Claim the login reward every day to keep your streak.

## Handy extras

- `/bp`: your backpack, extra storage you carry everywhere
- `/wallet`: your balance, CarbonCoins, vote points and crate keys
- `/crates`: warp to the crates and spend your keys
- `/discord`: the Discord invite
