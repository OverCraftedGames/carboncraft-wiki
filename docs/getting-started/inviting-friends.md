# Inviting friends

CarbonCorp's **Employee Referral Program™** rewards you for bringing friends to
CarbonCraft. The rewards come as your friend sticks around, and they get a welcome bonus
too.

## If a friend invited you

In your **first 3 hours** on the server, type:

```
/referredby <their name>
```

Then click the confirm message in chat. You can only name one person, and you can't
change it afterwards, so check the spelling.

The person you name must:

- have joined CarbonCraft before you
- have played for at least 2 hours
- not be on the same internet connection as you. If you live together, ask staff to
  link you.

Once you've played **2 hours** after linking, you get a **Carbon Key** as a welcome
bonus.

## If you invite a friend

Tell your friend to type `/referredby <your name>` within their first 3 hours. You're
paid as they reach each milestone:

| Milestone | Your friend has to | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Settled In | Play 2 hours | 1 Carbon Key (and they get one too) |
| Regular | Play 10 hours | 1 Plasma Key + a random [plushie](../cosmetics/plushies.md) |
| Voter | Reach 12 [votes](voting.md) | 1 Plasma Key + a random plushie |
| Supporter | Spend $10 or more in total in the [store](https://store.carboncraft.xyz) | 1 Iridium Key + a random plushie |

- Playtime counts from the moment they link to you. Time spent AFK doesn't count.
- If you're offline when a milestone is reached, your reward waits until you next join.
- A plushie needs a free inventory slot. If you're full, it waits and arrives within a
  minute of you making room.
- Every milestone pays once per friend, and there's no limit on how many friends you
  can invite.

!!! note "Alt accounts don't count"
    Second accounts on your own connection are blocked, and nothing is paid just for
    linking. Every reward needs a real player to keep playing.

## The referral menu

Type `/refer` (or `/referrals`) to open your referral menu. It shows:

- every friend you've recruited, with the milestones they've reached and how close they
  are to the next
- who recruited you, if anyone did
- how the program works, with every milestone and reward
- **Top Recruiters**: the five players with the most qualified referrals
