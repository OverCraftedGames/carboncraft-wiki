# Rules

These are the rules shown by `/rules` in game. New rules can be added for events and
updates, so check back now and then. Staff have the final say on every ruling,
including situations the rules don't cover.

## Chat

- Respect each other.
- All chat on the server must be in **English**.
- Keep conversation civil.
- Don't threaten players or staff.
- Be welcoming and positive.
- No racist, sexist or vulgar remarks.
- No links or images in server chat.

## Gameplay

- No modded clients other than OptiFine.
- No gameplay-altering mods.
- No teleport killing, spawn killing or traps.
- Keep redstone machines to a reasonable size.
- Report economy bugs to staff as soon as you find them. Abusing one gets you banned.

## Other

- No advertising or promotion.
- Don't ask for staff constantly.
- Don't set homes inside other players' land.
- Keep politics and controversy out of chat.
- Staff decisions are final. No ban evasion.

The full rule list is on [our Discord](https://discord.gg/carboncraft).
