# Voting & vote tiers

Voting is the main way to rank up on CarbonCraft, and it's free. Each vote helps the
server get found by new players, and pays you back.

## How to vote

Type `/vote` for the list of voting sites. There are **six**. You can vote on each one
once a day.

Each vote gives you:

- progress toward your next **vote tier**
- **vote points** to spend in `/voteshop`
- progress toward the server's **vote party**

## Vote tiers

Every **35 votes** moves you up one tier, and the rank-up happens automatically.
Every tier pays **$2,000** and **100 claim blocks**. Every fifth tier adds bigger perks
and a new chat suffix.

| Tier | Votes | Extra perks |
|---|---|---|
| I | 35 | 3 homes, 1 player warp, 5 auctions, single-use SafariNets |
| II–IV | 70–140 | 2 player warps at tier IV |
| **V** | 175 | 4 homes, 1 player vault, reusable SafariNets, `[V]` suffix |
| VI–IX | 210–315 | |
| **X** | 350 | 5 homes, 3 player warps, 10 auctions, 2 player vaults, **Nether RTP**, `[X]` suffix |
| XI–XIV | 385–490 | |
| **XV** | 525 | 6 homes, 4 player warps, 3 player vaults, `[XV]` suffix |
| XVI–XIX | 560–665 | |
| **XX** | 700 | 7 homes, 5 player warps, 4 player vaults, 15 auctions, **End RTP**, `[XX]` suffix |
| **XXV** | 875 | 8 homes, 6 player warps, 5 player vaults, `[XXV]` suffix |

The tiers continue past XXV. Type `/voting` to see every tier, what it unlocks and how
close you are to the next one.

## CarbonCoin week

Vote on **all six sites in one day** to claim that day's CarbonCoins. The reward
climbs each day:

| Day | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CarbonCoins | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 |

A perfect week pays **56 CarbonCoins**, then starts again at day 1. Miss a day and
the week resets.
