# Store rewards

Everything you buy on the [store](https://store.carboncraft.xyz) also counts towards
extra rewards on top of the package itself. They all go through one menu,
`/storerewards`.

- [Monthly rewards](monthly-rewards.md): bonuses for what you spend in a month, plus a
  server goal that pays everyone who plays
- [Supporter of the Month](supporter-of-the-month.md): the top three supporters each
  month, and the **[MVP™]** tag

Monthly rewards and Supporter of the Month start in **November 2026**.

## Claiming your rewards

Type `/storerewards` (or `/claimrewards`) to open your rewards chest.

- **Click a reward** to claim it, or **Claim All** to take everything that fits.
- Crate keys go straight into your account and need no space. Items like plushies need
  free inventory slots, and the reward shows how many.
- Rewards wait in the chest for as long as you like. You'll get a reminder in chat when
  you join with something unclaimed.
- Your head at the top shows your lifetime support, this month's total and your next
  monthly reward.

!!! tip "Rewards can take a few minutes"
    Your store package arrives instantly, but these extra rewards are checked every five
    minutes. If one hasn't shown up yet, give it a moment.

## Cosmetics

CarbonCoins from the store are spent in `/coinshop`. Tags and join messages are under
**Cosmetics**, and plushies have their own button next to it.

| Cosmetic | Price | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Tag packs, five tags each | 350 CarbonCoins | [Tags](../cosmetics/tags.md#tag-packs) |
| Movie night tags | 100 CarbonCoins each | [Tags](../cosmetics/tags.md#movie-night-tags) |
| Join messages | 500, 750 or 1,000 CarbonCoins | [Join messages](../cosmetics/join-messages.md) |
| Plushies | 100 to 200 CarbonCoins, packs 700 | [Plushies](../cosmetics/plushies.md) |

Particles are coming to the Cosmetics menu later.

Every purchase asks you to confirm before the coins are taken. Tags and join messages
you already own are marked **Owned** and can't be bought twice. Some can't be bought at
all and are earned by playing instead; each page lists how to get them.

## Seasonal offers

Some sales come with a bonus. Right now, any single purchase of **$19 or more** adds
the [Halloween Plushies Pack](../cosmetics/plushies.md) to your rewards chest: ten
plushies with 27 poses between them. It needs 10 free inventory slots to claim.
