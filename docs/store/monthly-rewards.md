# Monthly rewards

Starts in **November 2026**. Months run on UTC time, from the 1st to the last day.

## Your monthly rewards

What you spend in a month unlocks up to three rewards. Each one is paid once a month,
and they stack: reach $150 and you get all three.

| Spent this month | Reward | Free slots needed |
|---|---|---|
| $25 | 2 Plasma Keys | 0 |
| $75 | 2 Iridium Keys + a random [plushie](../cosmetics/plushies.md) | 1 |
| $150 | 4 Iridium Keys + 2 random plushies | 2 |

Your total resets on the 1st, and the rewards can be earned again. Your head in
`/storerewards` shows how far you are from the next one.

## Server goal

Every purchase from every player also counts towards a server-wide goal for the month.
When the server reaches a stage, **everyone who has played 3 hours that month** gets a
reward, whether they bought anything or not.

| Server total this month | Everyone with 3 hours played gets |
|---|---|
| $500 | 1 Carbon Key |
| $1,000 | 1 Plasma Key |
| $1,500 | 1 Iridium Key |

- Each stage is announced in chat when it's reached.
- Time spent AFK doesn't count towards the 3 hours.
- Reach 3 hours after a stage was hit and you still get it, as long as it's the same
  month.
- The beacon in `/storerewards` shows the current stage and your playtime.
