# Supporter of the Month

Starts with **November 2026**. The three players who spent the most on the store in a
month are announced to the server and rewarded.

| Place | Reward |
|---|---|
| #1 | The <span class="mc-tag"><span style="color:#555555">[</span><span style="color:#0B3D91;font-weight:700">M</span><span style="color:#2F649E;font-weight:700">V</span><span style="color:#548BAB;font-weight:700">P</span><span style="color:#FFFFFF">™</span><span style="color:#555555">]</span></span> tag, 5 Iridium Keys and a random [plushie](../cosmetics/plushies.md) |
| #2 | 3 Iridium Keys and a random plushie |
| #3 | 2 Iridium Keys and a random plushie |

## How it's decided

- Only that month's spending counts, and you need at least **$50** that month to place.
- The winners are picked **two days after the month ends**, so late payments still
  count.
- If two players spent the same, the one who got there first places higher.
- Rewards go to your `/storerewards` chest. The plushie needs one free inventory slot.

The **[MVP™]** tag is yours until the next month's MVP is announced, then it moves on.

## Opting out

Rather not be named? Open `/storerewards` and click the **Supporter of the Month**
name tag to hide yourself. You can switch back the same way. While hidden you can't
place, so the next player moves up.
